SHEIN: redesigning filters after 2 of 5 couldn’t finish

independent academic team study · Winter 2026

In a team usability study of 5 participants, only 2 fully completed filtering despite a positive SUS. I moderated sessions and designed a recoverable empty state, visible filter chips, and a size-aware quick add. The designs have not been tested.

Methods: moderated usability testing, think-aloud, SUS Formal mean SUS score: 81.5
