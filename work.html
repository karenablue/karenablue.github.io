SHEIN: designing a clearer path through filters and quick add

independent academic team study · Winter 2026

I designed visible filter chips, a recoverable empty state, and a size-aware quick add for desktop and mobile. The concepts respond to problems observed in our team usability study, where I moderated sessions. The redesigns have not been tested.

Design: desktop and mobile flows; component states Research: 5 formal usability sessions; mean SUS 81.5
