the short version:

I like making complicated things make sense.

I'm Karena, a UX researcher who also designs and codes. I pay attention to the distance between how a system is supposed to work and how people actually use it: a label that means something different to the reader, or a menu that hides the one link they came for.

I started in computer science. Along the way I got more interested in the people using software than in the software itself. I wanted to know how they read a page, which words they look for, and what makes a site feel trustworthy.

That’s why I’m now studying human–computer interaction. I still like building things. I just care more about whether they make sense to the person on the other side.

favorite kind of problem “Why is this harder than it needs to be?”

what I wonder about

Questions I keep coming back to

⌕

Behavior

What changes between what people say they will do, what they intend, and what they actually do when they use a system?

✦

Perception

What does someone expect a page to do before they’ve clicked anything?

♡

Identity + culture

How do a community’s words and norms change what feels welcoming or trustworthy to its members?

→

Clarity

Where is a site making people think harder than they should, and what would make the next step obvious?
